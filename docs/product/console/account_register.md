# Account Registration and Login

To use EasyIM, you first need to register an account. Your account is an important credential for logging in to [EasyIM Console](https://console.easyim.ai/user/login)) to configure and manage your app.

## Register an account

You can register an EasyIM account using your email address and a verification code:

1. Open the [EasyIM Console registration page](https://console.easyim.ai/user/register).
2. Enter your email address, click **Get Code**, and then enter the verification code you receive by email.
3. Select **I have read and agree to Service Terms and Privacy Policy**.
4. Click **Sign up**.
   
   ![img](/images/console/account_registration_code.png)

5. On the welcome page, select **Enterprise User** or **Individual User**, and enter the required information.
   The login password must contain 8–22 characters and at least two of the following character types: uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.

   ![img](/images/console/welcome.png)

## Log in to the console

You can log in to EasyIM Console using your email address and password:

1. Open the [EasyIM Console login page](https://console.easyim.ai/user/login).
2. Enter your email address and login password.
3. Select **I have read and agree to Service Terms and Privacy Policy**.
4. Click **Login** to log in to EasyIM Console.

![img](/images/console/account_login_email_pwd.png)
