# EasyIM SDK Compliance Guidelines

In accordance with Chinese laws, regulations, and regulatory requirements, app developers and operators (hereinafter referred to as "developers" or "you") must respect and protect end users' personal information when providing online products and services. You must not collect or use personal information in violation of laws or regulations. You must ensure and undertake that the processing of personal information has obtained authorization and consent from end users, follow the principle of data minimization, and implement effective technical and organizational measures to safeguard personal information.

To help developers better fulfill personal information protection requirements when using the SDK and avoid infringing on users' rights and interests in their personal information, Beijing Easemob Network Technology Co., Ltd. has prepared these EasyIM SDK Compliance Guidelines.

For details about compliant SDK usage, see the [Easemob official website](https://www.easemob.com/news/privacy).
