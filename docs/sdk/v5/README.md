SDK V5.0.0 currently fully supports Android, iOS, and Web. Support for additional platforms will be released in future versions. Stay tuned for updates.

## Supported platforms

| Platform | Minimum supported version | Source code | One-to-one and group chat UIKit | Chat room UIKit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Android](android/) | Android 5.0 or later (API level 21 or later) | Not available | Supported | Supported |
| [iOS](ios/) | iOS 10.0 or later | Not available | Supported | Supported |
| [Web](web/) | Internet Explorer 9 or later<br/>Firefox 10 or later<br/>Chrome 54 or later<br/>Safari 6 or later<br/>Edge 12 or later<br/>Opera 58 or later<br/>iOS Safari 7 or later<br/>Android Browser 4.4 (KitKat) or later | Not available | Supported | Supported |
